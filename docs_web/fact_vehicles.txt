TITLE: Cars and motorbikes on campus

Can a student bring a car or motorbike onto the University of Embu campus? Students must not keep motor vehicles or motor bikes on the University premises without prior written permission of the University Administration. Such permission is not given without proof of a current driving licence and a valid certificate of insurance. The permission may be refused or withdrawn without giving any reasons. (Student Handbook 2024/2025, section 5.2 General Conduct, page 48.)
