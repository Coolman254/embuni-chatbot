TITLE: Grading system and grade percentages

The University of Embu uses the Mark Grade System. A course mark ranges from 0 to 100 percent and comes from continuous assessment tests and end of semester examinations. The letter grades and their percentage ranges for undergraduate courses are: A is 70% and above; B is 60% and above but less than 70%; C is 50% and above but less than 60%; D is 40% and above but less than 50%; F is below 40%. The pass mark for taught Doctorate, Masters, medical courses and some programmes regulated by professional bodies is 50%. (Student Handbook 2024/2025, section 4.8 Grading System.)
