TITLE: Exam cheating penalty and exam room rules

What happens if a student cheats in an exam? What is the penalty for cheating? The penalty for cheating (examination malpractice) in an examination is expulsion from the University. Having a mobile phone or any unauthorised electronic device in the examination room is also treated as cheating and the penalty is expulsion from the University.
What to carry to the exam room: the Examination Card and the student ID card, both displayed on the desk throughout the examination.
Late arrival: no student is allowed to enter the examination room more than 30 minutes after the examination has started. Leaving early: no student may leave before 30 minutes have elapsed.
